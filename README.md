# Video Player & Reels Controls for Facebook™ — Support

Welcome to the official public support and privacy-information repository for **Video Player & Reels Controls for Facebook™**, a browser extension that adds enhanced controls for Facebook videos and Reels.

**This repository is for support and documentation only.** The extension's application source code is maintained privately; it is not published here.

## What the extension does

Video Player & Reels Controls for Facebook™ is designed to improve playback and multitasking when watching Facebook videos and Reels. Depending on the browser and available video streams, features include:

- HD video playback and quality selection
- Playback speed, seeking, volume boost, and keyboard shortcuts
- Floating mini-player / Picture-in-Picture support
- Split-screen video and comments
- User-initiated downloads of supported video content
- Resume playback and optional local watch history

Availability and behavior may vary by browser, Facebook content, and available media streams.

## Report a bug

Before submitting an issue, please [search existing reports](https://github.com/git-sujon/video-player-reels-controls-support/issues) to avoid duplicates.

Then [open a new issue](https://github.com/git-sujon/video-player-reels-controls-support/issues/new) with:

1. **Extension version** (if known)
2. **Browser and version** (Chrome or Firefox) and operating system
3. **Where it happened** (Facebook video, Watch page, or Reel)
4. **Player mode** (regular player, in-site mini-player, or Picture-in-Picture)
5. **Steps to reproduce**
6. **Expected result and actual result**
7. **Error message or a redacted screenshot**, if helpful

If the issue concerns HD playback or downloads, mention the selected quality and whether other supported videos work. **Do not post a private video link or a signed stream URL.**

## Basic troubleshooting

1. Update the browser and extension to the latest available version.
2. Reload the Facebook page and try the video or Reel again.
3. Check the extension's player preferences and playback controls.
4. Test another video you are authorized to access to see whether the issue is content-specific.
5. If the issue persists, submit a report with the details above.

Some playback, quality, comments, and download features depend on Facebook's available streams, your access to the content, and browser capabilities.

## Privacy and data handling

The extension stores player settings locally. **Watch history and resume positions are enabled by default**, but can be disabled using the extension popup. A separate mini-player handoff record may be stored locally to restore playback after navigation, even if history or resume saving is disabled.

The extension communicates with Facebook and its media delivery services to play videos, retrieve comments and subtitles, and prefetch supported content. It does not use developer-operated analytics or advertising trackers, according to the published policy.

**Read the complete [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md)** for the exact storage, retention, private-browsing, permission, and network-request disclosures.

### Please protect your privacy when reporting issues

**GitHub issues and comments are public. Do not share:**

- Passwords, cookies, access tokens, or session information
- Signed or expiring media URLs or manifests
- Private Facebook posts, conversations, or personal information
- Screenshots or logs containing unredacted personal data

A description of the problem is enough to begin troubleshooting.
